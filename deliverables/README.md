open it with your device's video player. The ZIP includes editable sources,
original media, dependency lockfile, placement settings, and render scripts.
Extract it and follow LECTURE.md inside the password-lecture folder.

The lecturer, logo, and original background remain visible, with animations
confined to the empty white area. Video metadata, full decode, original audio
